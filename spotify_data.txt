About
15 monthly listeners
Since 2015, Mouje's sound took major and various twists and turns, from raging basses to ethereal melodies and synth textures; The Amman, Jordan-based artist draws inspiration from a lot of local as well as global acts, crafting his own world and introducing the essence of Electronic Music in his native Arabic language. When Tajreeh was released in 2018, it was the track symbolizing his growth, his evolving sound, and a chance to break free from the constraints of being trapped by generic rhythms and the lifeless nature of dry basses, breaking down into the interlude for his atmospheric and sensational soundscapes, topped with immersive songwriting, it was an introduction to a new era of Mouje. Introducing his new sound with Rah Telhaqni from his upcoming album Xetopia, it was the track to capture youth, friendship, trust, and companionship. The track features captivating vocals by the talented vocalist Desana and an uplifting impact of synths and sounds drawn from his wide inspirations showcasing his new sonic world. Mouje is currently working on his debut album "Xetopia" expected to release soon.
